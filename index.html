Astro privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Astro: Stargazing Forecast (Android)

Astro has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The developer receives none of your data.

Location

You add observing sites using your phone's location or by typing coordinates. Sites are stored on your phone. To get forecasts, a site's coordinates are sent to the weather services Open-Meteo and 7Timer!. For the cloud loop, a region around the site, rounded to whole degrees, is sent to NASA GIBS or, for sites in Europe, Africa and western Asia, EUMETSAT. These services receive each request like any website visit, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

About once a day Astro also downloads a small settings file from GitHub Pages, the service that hosts this page, which says which weather services to use. That request contains no location; GitHub receives your IP address under its privacy statement.

Camera

When you trace a skyline, the camera image is analysed on your phone. Only an outline of how high obstructions reach is saved, on the phone. No photo is stored or sent anywhere.

Sky photos

Opening an object sends only that object's position in the sky to NASA SkyView to fetch a survey photo.

Alerts

If you turn on go-night alerts, Astro checks the forecast for your selected site in the afternoon, using the same weather requests. Alerts are created on the phone.

Backup

If Android backup is on, your settings, sites and observing log are included in your own Google backup. Skyline outlines are not.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Astro, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it holds.

Children

Astro is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: info@jecam.com.
